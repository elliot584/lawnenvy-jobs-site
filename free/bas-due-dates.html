Good to know

Lodging online, paying late and annual returns.

If you lodge online you may be eligible for an extra 2 weeks to lodge and pay your quarterly BAS, and you may get extra time if you lodge through a registered tax or BAS agent. A later date does not apply to Quarter 2 because its due date already includes a one-month extension.

  • Pay in full and on time to avoid general interest charge (GIC).
  • The ATO app can set calendar reminders for lodgment dates.
  • If you can't pay on time, contact the ATO or your tax professional before the due date. If you owe $200,000 or less you may be able to set up a payment plan online.
  • Businesses that are voluntarily registered for GST with GST turnover under $75,000 ($150,000 for non-profits) lodge an annual GST return, due 31 October (or 28 February if they are not required to lodge a tax return then).

This page lists the dates for 2026-27 only. When the last of them has passed it says so rather than guessing the next year's dates.

Questions

Good to know.

When is my next BAS due?

The boxes at the top of this page work it out from today's date. Quarterly BAS for 2026-27 is due 28 October 2026, 28 February 2027 (a Sunday, so you have until the next business day), 28 April 2027 and 28 July 2027. Monthly BAS is due on the 21st day of the month after the reporting period.

Do I report quarterly or monthly?

You report quarterly if your GST turnover is under $20 million and the ATO hasn't told you to report monthly. You must report monthly if your GST turnover is $20 million or more or the ATO has directed you to; smaller businesses can choose to.

What if the due date is on a weekend or public holiday?

You have until the next business day to lodge and pay.

Do I get extra time if I lodge online?

If you lodge online you may be eligible for an extra 2 weeks to lodge and pay your quarterly BAS, and you may get extra time if you lodge through a registered tax or BAS agent. A later date does not apply to Quarter 2 because its due date already includes a one-month extension.
