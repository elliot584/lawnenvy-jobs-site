Read this first

What the checklist is, and what it is not.

This is a working checklist, not a guarantee that a bond is returned. The standard that counts is the property's entry condition report and what the agent or owner asks for. Ask for a copy of the entry condition report before you price the job, and fit the lines to it.

In Queensland, the Residential Tenancies Authority's Exit condition report (Form 14a) records the condition of the rental premises at the end of the tenancy. The RTA says the Entry condition report (Form 1a) is compared to it at the end of the tenancy.

The RTA's cleaning page says fair wear and tear does not include dirt, grime or grease, and suggests going room by room so nothing is missed.

Other states and territories have their own forms: check your state's tenancy authority.

General information, not legal advice. Checked against the RTA pages on 15 November 2026.

On the day

How to work through a bond clean.

  1. Before you price it. Read the entry condition report and ask the agent or owner what they want done. Remove the lines for rooms the property does not have, and add the extras.
  2. Take before photos. One or two of each room before you start, so you can show what you walked into.
  3. Top to bottom, one room at a time. Ceiling fans and light fittings first, floors last. Tick each line as you finish it. If you did not do it, do not tick it.
  4. Write down what you could not fix. A stain that will not lift or a mark that was there before goes in the notes box.
  5. Walk through at the end. Go room by room with the entry condition report in hand, take the after photos, and save the checklist as a PDF for the job.
Questions

Good to know.

Who is this checklist for?

Cleaners doing an end of lease clean, often called a bond clean. It is a working list for the job: tick each line on your phone as you go, or print it and tick it by hand.

Is the bond clean checklist free?

Yes. There is nothing to sign up for. Fill it in, then print it or save it as a PDF.

Does it keep what I type?

No. It all stays in this page in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere or saved, so reloading the page clears it. Save a PDF of the checklist before you leave.

Can I change the rooms and lines?

Yes. Rename, move or remove any line, and add your own, up to 20 lines in each room. Remove every line in a room the property does not have and that room is left off the printed checklist.
