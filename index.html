Questions

Good to know.

Is this only for lawn mowing?

No — it covers lawn care, garden maintenance, hedging, pressure washing and turf/lawn renovation work. If your business quotes and schedules outdoor jobs, it fits.

Do I need to migrate my existing clients?

You can import your customer list from a CSV export — QuickBooks, Xero, Jobber, a spreadsheet, most formats work — in a couple of clicks, or just add clients as new jobs come in.

Is my data private from other businesses using the app?

Yes — every business's data is fully isolated. Nobody else using the software can see your jobs, clients or numbers.

What will it cost?

From $55/mo flat for a solo operator with up to 2 staff, up to $229/mo for unlimited staff — see the full pricing. Solo operators get 3 months free to try it with real data before paying anything, no card required.
