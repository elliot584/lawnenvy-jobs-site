Why not just use the free one?

Because most "free" isn't really free — it's a funnel. Those tiers cap out on clients, jobs or trades right when your business is actually growing, and the ones that stay free long-term make it back some other way: ads, upsells, or your data being the product.

Kept has a real free plan — no time limit, no card, no catch. It's capped at 15 clients and 15 jobs/quotes a month, which is plenty to run a genuinely small operation on for as long as you want. When you outgrow that, upgrading is one click from Settings — not a forced decision on someone else's clock.

What trades does this actually cover?

Lawn care, garden maintenance and turf renovation, residential and commercial cleaning, pressure washing and exterior cleaning, plus over 20 other service trades already built in. Don't see yours — use the "request your trade" section above and we'll build it out.

Do I need to migrate my existing clients?

You can import your customer list from a CSV export — QuickBooks, Xero, Jobber, a spreadsheet, most formats work — in a couple of clicks, or just add clients as new jobs come in.

Is my data private from other businesses using the app?

Yes — every business's data is fully isolated. Nobody else using the software can see your jobs, clients or numbers.

What will it cost?

Nothing to start — every business begins on a free plan, no card required. From there it's $35/mo + GST for 1 person, priced by team size, up to $229/mo + GST at 11 people — bigger teams are priced individually. See the full pricing.
