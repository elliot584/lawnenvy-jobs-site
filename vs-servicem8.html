Kept vs ServiceM8

Kept and ServiceM8 both have a free plan. Kept is built around lawn care, cleaning, pressure washing and handyman work.

ServiceM8 is job-management software for tradies and service businesses, with plans based on jobs a month and an SMS allowance. Kept covers the same ground and adds trade-specific calculators and weather blockout, which we didn't find on ServiceM8's feature pages.

What reviewers say

What reviewers say about ServiceM8.

Mostly positive reviews

ServiceM8 is rated 4.5 out of 5 on Capterra Australia (311 reviews, 291 of them four or five stars), 4.6 on the Xero App Store (351 reviews) and 4.1 on Trustpilot (119 reviews). Many of the positive reviews praise how easy it is to use, the time it saves and the support team. In the one-to-three-star reviews we read, support is the most common complaint on Trustpilot (12 of 26) and Capterra (9 of 20): slow or generic replies and no phone line. On the Xero App Store the most common are the limits of the Android app and Xero sync problems.

One app for many trades

ServiceM8 has industry pages for lawn care, cleaning and others, but we found no soil, turf, bond-clean or house-wash calculators on its feature pages. Kept includes them.

Reviews read on 24 October 2026: all 351 on the Xero App Store (Australia), 118 of the 119 on Trustpilot (April 2023 to October 2026, including all 26 rated one to three stars) and 304 of the 311 on Capterra Australia (including all 20 rated one to three stars). Many of the critical reviews are several years old, and individual experiences vary.

Questions

Good to know.

Do both Kept and ServiceM8 have a free plan?

Yes, both do. Kept's Free plan is capped at 15 clients and 15 jobs/quotes a month with no card required. ServiceM8's Free plan is for one user and 30 new jobs a month (it adds bonus jobs when you take card payments through ServiceM8), with SMS at 10c each (checked October 2026).

Does ServiceM8 have trade-specific calculators?

We found none on ServiceM8's feature pages (checked October 2026). ServiceM8 is general trades software, with industry pages for lawn care, cleaning and others. Kept includes a turf/reno soil calculator, bond-clean and house-wash calculators, and AU-native GST/BAS tools built in.
