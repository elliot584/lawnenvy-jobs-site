Questions

Good to know.

Do both Kept and ServiceM8 have a free plan?

Yes, both do. Kept's Free plan is capped at 15 clients and 15 jobs/quotes a month with no card required; ServiceM8's free tier has its own limits on jobs and users.

Does ServiceM8 have trade-specific calculators?

No — ServiceM8 is general trades software. Kept includes a turf/reno soil calculator, bond-clean and house-wash calculators, and AU-native GST/BAS tools built in.
