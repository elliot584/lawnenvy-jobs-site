Questions

Good to know.

Can I send a subcontractor a work order without giving them full access?

Yes — a no-login PDF work order can be texted or emailed straight to a sub, without handing them access to your books or client list.

Does it handle jobs that run over several days?

Yes — break a bigger job into dated task cards spread across days, then bill it all as one invoice or in stages as it progresses.

Can I track what materials a job actually used?

Log what a job used — timber, fittings, whatever your trade runs on — against quantity, so job costing means more than just hours on the tools.
