Sub-processors

Last updated: 23 August 2026

Kept is operated by Lawn Envy Australia (ABN 19 863 392 800). Running the service means using a small number of other companies. This page lists every one of them, what they do, and what they can actually see — published because the Privacy Policy should not have to say "third-party services" and leave you guessing.

Read the "what they can see" column carefully. Your jobs, clients, quotes and invoices are encrypted on your device before they leave it. Where a provider below holds that data, it holds unreadable ciphertext — not a copy of your business. The information that is readable is your account and business contact details, and, if you use the marketplace, the job requests submitted to you.

Overseas disclosure

Under Australian Privacy Principle 8 we have to tell you when personal information may go overseas, and where. Your primary datastore is in Sydney, so the bulk of what we hold about you — including marketplace job requests — stays in Australia.

The providers marked above as operating outside Australia may store or process information overseas, in the countries listed. We choose providers we consider reputable and rely on the contractual privacy commitments each of them publishes. Where the information involved is your encrypted business data, an overseas provider holds only ciphertext.

Changes

If we add or replace a sub-processor we will update this page and the date at the top. If a change materially affects how your personal information is handled, we will say so in the app rather than relying on you to check.
