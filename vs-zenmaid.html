Questions

Does Kept's price go up per cleaner like ZenMaid's?

No — Kept is priced by seat band (1 person, 2–4, 5–10, 11+), so the price only changes at those thresholds, not every time you add one more cleaner.

Does Kept have a bond-clean calculator like ZenMaid?

Yes — room and surface-based pricing for regular and bond cleans is built straight into the quote.
