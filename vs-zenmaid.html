Priced by seat band, not by every cleaner you add.

ZenMaid charges a monthly fee for every active user besides the owner: US$4 on Starter, US$14 on Pro and US$24 on Pro Max, on top of the base price (its help centre, checked October 2026). Kept's price only changes at seat-band thresholds — and includes a bond-clean calculator built in.

What reviewers say

What reviewers say about ZenMaid.

Mostly positive on Capterra

Capterra shows ZenMaid at 4.7 out of 5 from 205 reviews (checked October 2026). Nine of the ten most recent reviews we read (October 2025 to April 2026) gave 5 stars, praising ease of use and support. Three say the price is fair or good value, and three mention occasional glitches.

Weaker on Trustpilot and the iPhone app store

Trustpilot shows 2.1 from 9 reviews, seven of them 1-star, mostly about support, bugs and features. A smaller number of reviewers raise cost: one Trustpilot review (July 2020) priced four staff at about £80 a month, and two reviews of ZenMaid's iPhone app (September 2025 and January 2026) say their bill rose unexpectedly.

Built for cleaning

Reviewers like that ZenMaid is made just for cleaning companies (three of those ten Capterra reviews say so). If the business ever expands into another trade, Kept already covers 20+ trades in the same app.

Questions

Good to know.

Does Kept's price go up per cleaner like ZenMaid's?

No — Kept is priced by seat band (1 person, 2–4, 5–10, 11+), so the price only changes at those thresholds, not every time you add one more cleaner. ZenMaid adds a monthly fee for every extra active user: US$4 on Starter, US$14 on Pro and US$24 on Pro Max (checked October 2026).

Does Kept have a bond-clean calculator?

Yes — room and surface-based pricing for regular and bond cleans is built straight into the quote.
