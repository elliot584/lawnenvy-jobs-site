Questions

Good to know.

Does it handle recurring mow rounds?

Yes — set a job to repeat weekly, fortnightly or monthly and it keeps generating until you cancel the series. A reschedule or a rain-day move only affects that one visit, not the whole round.

What does the soil calculator actually do?

As you build a turf renovation or garden reno quote, it works out the m³ of soil needed live from the area and depth you enter, so you're not doing that math in a separate spreadsheet or guessing on the day.

Can it optimise my run order for the day?

Yes, on the Team plan and above — a day's stops get reordered automatically for the shortest run, not just the order jobs were booked in.
