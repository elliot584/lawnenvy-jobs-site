Marketplace Terms

Last updated: 22 October 2026

These terms cover the request-a-quote marketplace — the form where you describe a job and we pass it to nearby businesses so they can quote on it. They are for the person requesting the work. If you are a business using Kept to run your operations, the Terms of Service apply to you instead.

The one thing to understand: Kept introduces you to businesses. It is not the tradesperson. Any work you agree to is a contract between you and that business — they set the price, they do the work, and they are responsible for it. We are not a party to it and we do not take a cut of it.

1. Who this is with

The marketplace is operated by Lawn Envy Australia (ABN 19 863 392 800), trading as Kept.

2. What happens when you submit a request

  • You tell us the type of work, your suburb and postcode, a description, and how to contact you.
  • We show the request to businesses registered in that trade, and we email a few of those whose service area covers your suburb — or, if too few do, a few further away — to tell them about it.
  • Those businesses can choose whether to quote. We cap how many quotes a single request can receive (normally four) so you are not buried in them.
  • Quotes come back to you on a private page, and we email you when quotes arrive if you gave us an email address. Nothing is booked until you accept a quote, and you can accept one or none of them.

3. What we share, and with whom

While businesses decide whether to quote, they can see the type of work, your suburb and postcode, and the description you wrote. They cannot see your name, address, phone number or email. Because they can read the description, please leave your phone number and address out of it.

When you accept a quote, the business that sent it receives your name, address, phone number and email, so it can contact you and arrange the job. Businesses you did not choose never receive them.

We do not sell your information, we do not share it for anyone else's marketing, and we do not show your request to businesses outside the trade you asked for.

4. No guarantees

  • We cannot guarantee you will receive any quotes, or a particular number of them. That depends on which businesses are active in your area.
  • We do not set, review or negotiate prices.
  • We do not verify the licensing, insurance, qualifications or quality of the businesses on the marketplace. Check licences and insurance yourself before engaging anyone, particularly for electrical, plumbing, gas or other licensed work.
  • Disputes about the work, the price or the outcome are between you and the business. We will help put you back in touch where we reasonably can, but we cannot resolve them for you.

5. Your information and your choices

You can ask us to withdraw a request or delete your details at any time — email support@keptsoftware.com.au. Withdrawing a request also stops the emails we send you about it.

Withdrawing a request stops us showing it to any further businesses. It cannot un-show it to businesses that have already seen it, but they never receive your contact details unless you accepted their quote. If you did accept a quote, that business already has your details, and withdrawing the request does not oblige it to delete them — you would need to ask it directly.

6. Where your information is stored

Marketplace requests are stored in our primary database in Sydney, Australia. The full list of service providers involved in running the platform is on the Sub-processors page, and the Privacy Policy explains how to access or correct your information and how to complain.

7. How long we keep it

A request and its contact details are kept while it is live and for a reasonable period afterwards so the business you chose can follow up and so we can deal with any complaint about it. After that it is deleted. You can ask for deletion sooner at any time.

8. Fair use

Submit genuine requests for work you actually want done. Don't submit requests on someone else's behalf without their knowledge, don't use the form to advertise to businesses, and don't submit false or abusive content. We may decline or remove a request that breaches this.

9. Your rights under Australian law

Nothing here limits rights you have under the Australian Consumer Law or the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth). If we have not resolved a privacy concern to your satisfaction, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.
