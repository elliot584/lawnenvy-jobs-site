What's actually in it

Everything a cleaning business runs on, already built.

Quotes → jobs → invoices

One record flows into the next — no retyping the same job three times.

Recurring jobs

Weekly, fortnightly or monthly, repeating forever until you cancel the series.

Multi-day projects

Break a big commercial contract into dated task cards spread across days, then bill it all as one invoice — or bill it in stages as it progresses.

Route optimisation

A day's stops reordered automatically for the shortest run, not just entry order.

Weather blockout

Flags likely-rain days for any outdoor-affected jobs and suggests the next clear one, one tap to move a day's jobs.

Regular & bond-clean calculators

Live pricing for regular cleans and bond/end-of-lease jobs as you build a quote — no separate spreadsheet.

GST/BAS built in, AU-native

Bank reconciliation

A clean P&L, GST position and mileage summary, ready to print or email off at tax time.

Equipment maintenance log

Client & lead CRM

Staff & payroll

Questions

Good to know.

Is this only for regular house cleaning?

No — it covers regular cleans, bond/end-of-lease cleans, commercial cleaning contracts and general residential cleaning work. If your business quotes and schedules cleaning jobs, it fits.

Do I need to migrate my existing clients?

You can import your customer list from a CSV export — QuickBooks, Xero, Jobber, a spreadsheet, most formats work — in a couple of clicks, or just add clients as new jobs come in.

Is my data private from other businesses using the app?

Yes — every business's data is fully isolated. Nobody else using the software can see your jobs, clients or numbers.

What will it cost?

From $55/mo flat for a solo operator with up to 2 staff, up to $229/mo for unlimited staff — see the full pricing. Solo operators get 3 months free to try it with real data before paying anything, no card required.
