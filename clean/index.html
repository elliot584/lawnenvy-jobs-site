Questions

Good to know.

How does the bond-clean calculator work?

Room and surface-based pricing built straight into the quote — enter the property's rooms and surfaces and the price builds live, instead of working it out on a separate spreadsheet or guessing.

Can I roster staff to different jobs?

Yes — staff rostering and payroll works from the Free plan onward: log hours, assign jobs to specific cleaners, and run pay with tax and super estimates worked out for you. How many staff you can add grows as you move up from Free through Crew, Team and Operation.

Are before/after photos useful for bond disputes?

Photos taken on the job are kept against that job for good — proof of a finished clean if a property manager or landlord ever queries the standard of work.
