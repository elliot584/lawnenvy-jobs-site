What reviewers say

The most-cited complaint about Jobber.

Accounting sync drops line items

Reviewers report Jobber's accounting sync drops line items on an estimated ~2% of syncs, making manual reconciliation a routine part of the job rather than the exception.

Per-user pricing scales against you

Team plans are priced per user, so the cost climbs every time you add a person — the opposite of Kept's seat-band pricing, which only changes at specific team-size thresholds.

Questions

Good to know.

Is Kept cheaper than Jobber?

For a small AU business, usually yes. Jobber is priced per user on its team plans in US dollars; Kept is priced in AUD by seat band, starting free, from $35/mo for one person up to $229/mo for 11+ people.

Does Jobber have trade-specific tools like Kept?

No — Jobber is general field-service software, not built around any one trade. Kept includes a turf/reno soil calculator, bond-clean and house-wash calculators, and AU-native GST/BAS tools out of the box.
